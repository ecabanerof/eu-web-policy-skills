# European jurisdiction guide

Use this guide to find official legislation and authorities before drafting terms, a privacy notice or a cookie policy.
Coverage is a research workflow for each applicable market.
Record the current rules for the specific site, activity and publication date.

## Start with the operator and its markets

1. Identify the operator's establishment and the countries or territories the site targets.
   For a transaction, identify the seller and contracting entity separately.
   Check relevant activity such as offers, delivery, language, marketing and monitoring before deciding where rules apply.
2. Record the applicable regimes separately for terms, privacy and terminal access.
   An operator in one country can have obligations in another.
   Distinguish rules tied to the seller, the consumer, the processing or the user's device.
3. Verify current legislation, effective dates, the competent authority and the publication language in each applicable market.
   Check the authoritative language version where an English translation is informational.
   Record each missing rule or implementation fact as a publication prerequisite.
4. Draft in English.
   Use market-specific modules if requirements differ and flag any required local-language versions for publication.

## European Union

The [27 EU Member States](https://european-union.europa.eu/principles-countries-history/eu-countries_en) are Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain and Sweden.

- Find EU law and notified national measures in [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/).
- Open the official national legislation gateway through [N-Lex](https://n-lex.europa.eu/n-lex/index).
- Find privacy authorities through the [EDPB member directory](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/our-members_en).
- Start a country-specific consumer review with the [European Commission's consumer contract law directory](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/consumer-protection-law/consumer-contract-law_en).
  Verify the national text and regulator guidance for each relevant market.

## European Economic Area outside the EU

The [EEA EFTA states](https://www.efta.int/eea-relations-eu/eea-institutions-two-pillar-structure/eea-efta-states) are Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway.
Check [EEA incorporation](https://www.efta.int/eealaw) and any adaptations and entry-into-force dates for each relevant act.
Verify implementation through [Iceland's Parliament](https://www.althingi.is/lagasafn/), [Liechtenstein's legal database](https://www.gesetze.li/konso/suche), or [Norway's legal database](https://lovdata.no/), and the applicable local authority.

## United Kingdom and Switzerland

| Market | Official starting points |
| --- | --- |
| United Kingdom | [Legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/), [government distance-selling guidance](https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses) and the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/) |
| Switzerland | [Fedlex](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/), [Federal SME Portal e-commerce guidance](https://www.kmu.admin.ch/en/statutory-obligations-swiss-and-european-e-commerce-laws) and the [Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/en) |

Check territorial extent and separate local regimes for dependencies and territories.
For example, confirm whether a UK rule applies in the market at issue before using it for Jersey, Guernsey, the Isle of Man or Gibraltar.
Check separate territorial rules when a market such as the Faroe Islands is in scope.
Check whether EU or EEA rules also apply to a UK or Swiss operator's activities in those markets.
