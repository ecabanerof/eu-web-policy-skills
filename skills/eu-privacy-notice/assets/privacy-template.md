# Privacy policy and short notices drafting template

Use only sections relevant to the processing the operator actually carries out.
Replace double braces with confirmed details and keep gaps outside the proposed notice.
Use plain language and place the most relevant information first.

## Who handles your information

{{Legal controller name}} is responsible for the personal information described in this notice.
For questions about your information or to exercise your rights, contact {{verified channel}}.

Include the representative, data protection officer or joint-controller information where applicable.

## What we collect and why

Use one entry per processing purpose.
Brief entries work for simple sites, and a table can help when the site has several distinct activities.

| What we use and where it comes from | Why we use it | Legal basis | Who receives it | How long we keep it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| {{categories and source}} | {{specific purpose}} | {{verified legal basis and any legitimate interest}} | {{recipients or categories and relevant roles}} | {{period or criteria}} |

For example, a contact section can start with “When you email us, we use the information in your message to respond.”
Add a separate entry for any further purpose such as newsletters, website security, or optional analytics.
Use the actual processing map to decide whether each activity exists and what basis applies.

Add the applicable disclosures for required data, indirect collection, or decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.
If data goes outside the EEA, identify the relevant countries or recipients and the applicable transfer mechanism and how safeguards can be obtained or consulted where required.

## Your choices and rights

Explain the applicable rights and how to exercise them.
For a consent-based purpose, say how to withdraw consent using the actual control.
For other rights, give a usable contact path.

> You can lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, including in the EU country where you usually live, work or believe the infringement occurred.

Use the complaint wording when the GDPR applies.
Adapt the reference to the relevant jurisdiction, including the EEA where applicable, and verify any named authority.

## Short notice at a collection point

Use this beside an active form or other direct collection point.
Link to the complete notice where the person provides their data and identify information important to that particular collection.

> {{Controller name}} uses the details you provide to {{purpose}}.
> {{How to exercise the relevant rights}}.
> Read our {{link to full privacy notice}} for the legal basis, retention, and other details.

If the form includes an optional newsletter or advertising choice, use a distinct choice and explain that purpose separately.
Check that the short notice matches the actual fields and submission behavior.
