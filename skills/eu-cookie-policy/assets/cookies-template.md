## Preferences panel

Include a separate choice for each optional purpose.
For each purpose, explain what is enabled, who provides it and what happens if the user leaves it off.
Keep optional choices off initially when prior consent is required.
Provide access to the full notice and a way to save or reject the available optional choices.

Compare all copy against the observed behavior on first visit, after rejection, after each granular choice and after withdrawal.
Keep any missing runtime evidence outside the notice and mark the draft as pending verification.
