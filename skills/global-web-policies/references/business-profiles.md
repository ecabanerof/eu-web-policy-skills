# Business profiles for global sites

Use the profiles that match the site's actual features.
Combine overlapping profiles into one market matrix and keep one row per distinct activity, responsible entity and relevant rule.
Draft the shared sections and any local variants with the [policy pack template](../assets/policy-pack-template.md).
Check the relevant markets in [market-sources.md](market-sources.md).

## SaaS

- **Terms.** Map each contracting entity and plan to its markets, audience, sign-up route, billing cycle, renewal and cancellation flow.
  Verify trial conversion, disclosures, statutory rights, support promises and what happens to access, export and deletion.
  Separate verified business and consumer terms where the rules differ.
- **Privacy.** Map own account, billing and security activities separately from customer-managed workspace data.
  Verify the role of each entity for each purpose, who receives or hosts the data, retention, transfers, integrations and where people exercise rights.
  Flag applicable processing agreements as a separate deliverable.
- **Cookies.** Inventory the public site, sign-in and authenticated workspaces.
  Check the classification and choice controls for session operations, preferences, embedded support and optional product analytics in each market.

Use local variants where a different entity sells the plan, the consumer's cancellation process changes, or the control differs by market.

## AI services

- **Terms.** Identify the feature, role of each provider or operator, input permissions, output terms and supported uses.
  Verify relevant disclosures, labelling and consumer protection requirements and locate them in the actual interface.
  For an EU market, check [AI Act Article 50](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act) where relevant.
- **Privacy.** Map prompts, uploads, outputs, feedback and usage data by plan and processing route.
  Verify what external models and connected tools receive, the entity and role, data location, staff or vendor review, retention and deletion.
  Separate delivering the feature from safety, evaluation, training and other secondary purposes.
  Verify any profiling or automated-decision rules in the applicable market and identify the rights and controls triggered.
- **Cookies.** Check browser operations at page load, widget launch and request submission.
  Record device access and optional telemetry by purpose and domain and check the applicable consent or opt-out behavior.
  Review data sent to a backend or external model as a separate privacy flow.

Put any unresolved training claim or transfer mechanism on hold for the affected plan and market.

## E-commerce

- **Terms.** Identify the seller, platform and checkout operator per market and offer.
  Verify disclosures, price and currency, delivery, fulfilment, cancellation or withdrawal, refunds, guarantees and complaints for the goods, services or digital content actually sold.
- **Privacy.** Map customer and guest checkout, payments, fraud prevention, order fulfilment, returns, marketing and advertising by purpose and entity.
  Verify what payment details the seller can access, what goes to processors or independent recipients, and the retention and transfers for each flow.
- **Cookies.** Inventory product pages, cart, hosted checkout, redirects and confirmation.
  Separate functional operations from attribution, conversion measurement and remarketing.
  Trace any server-side attribution or conversion event back to the browser identifiers it uses.
  Check prior consent, refusal, advertising opt-out or browser preference signals where applicable in each market and verify what happens across domains.

Keep seller and controller identities, local rights, price disclosures and choice controls consistent with the actual offer and jurisdiction.

## Creative and professional sites

Use this profile for artists, musicians, photographers, studios and independent professionals.
Combine it with e-commerce for actual sales or paid bookings.

- **Terms.** Separate public portfolio and press use, project enquiries, binding bookings and event ticket sales.
  Verify who owns or can license the work, who contracts, who organises an event and who sells tickets.
  Check local trader or professional disclosures and the applicable rules for deposits, cancellations and consumer rights.
- **Privacy.** Map enquiries, booking and press contacts, mailing lists, galleries and identifiable media by purpose and responsible entity.
  Separate client delivery, public portfolio use and promotion.
  Verify local image or release rules alongside the applicable data protection basis and check gallery access, hosting, transfers and retention.
- **Cookies.** Distinguish direct external links from embedded players, feeds, gallery tools, schedulers and ticket or payment widgets.
  Inventory what loads on first visit and on activation, and verify the choice or refusal controls on each relevant domain and market.

Keep a globally visible portfolio's actual markets and processing in the matrix.
Apply sales terms only to the transactions the relevant party actually offers.

## Restaurants and food sales

- **Terms.** Classify menus, reservations, catering, takeaway, delivery and packaged sales per market.
  Verify the operator and seller, reservation charges, mandatory food and allergen information, checkout and delivery conditions.
  Verify local withdrawal exceptions and separate remedies for delivery or product problems.
  Use the [EU food information regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1169/oj) or [UK Food Standards Agency guidance](https://www.food.gov.uk/business-guidance/allergen-guidance-for-food-businesses) where applicable and check the relevant food authority in other markets.
- **Privacy.** Verify the roles and flows between the restaurant or seller, reservation and ordering platforms, payment providers and delivery partners.
  Check whether dietary notes trigger special legal conditions and verify access, retention and any transfer.
- **Cookies.** Inventory maps, reservation and delivery widgets, and any hosted ordering flow.
  Verify the behavior and available choice controls by domain and market.

Combine this profile with e-commerce when the operator offers sales or paid bookings.
Keep food safety claims and publication language specific to the product and market.
