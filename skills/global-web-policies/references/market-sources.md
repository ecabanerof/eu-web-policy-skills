# Official starting points for global web policies

Starting points checked on 9 November 2026.
Check the current authoritative text, effective date and regulator guidance for each applicable market before drafting.
Use an official translation where available and verify the authoritative text when a translation is informational.

## Find the rules for each market

- Find the official legislation portal, privacy regulator, consumer authority and electronic communications regulator as relevant.
  The [UNCTAD Global Cyberlaw Tracker](https://unctad.org/topic/ecommerce-and-digital-economy/ecommerce-law-reform/summary-adoption-e-commerce-legislation-worldwide) and [consumer protection map](https://unctad.org/topic/competition-and-consumer-protection/consumer-protection-map) can help discover sources for additional markets.
  Verify any result against the current local law.
- For sales, verify seller disclosure, pre-contract and checkout information, renewal, returns, conformity remedies, and complaint routes.
  Check relevant state, provincial and territorial rules in addition to national law.
- For privacy, verify applicability, responsible entities, information duties, legal grounds or permissions, rights, transfers, and direct marketing.
  For tracking, check terminal-access rules and personal-data rules separately when the law distinguishes them.
  Check consent, refusal, targeted advertising, sale or sharing, and preference signals only where the applicable rules trigger them.

## Europe

- Use the [European jurisdiction guide](https://github.com/ecabanerof/eu-web-policy-skills/blob/main/JURISDICTIONS.md) to distinguish EU Member States, the EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other European markets.
  For the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, use the applicable [terms](https://github.com/ecabanerof/eu-web-policy-skills/blob/main/skills/eu-website-terms/references/legal-basis.md), [privacy](https://github.com/ecabanerof/eu-web-policy-skills/blob/main/skills/eu-privacy-notice/references/legal-basis.md) and [cookie](https://github.com/ecabanerof/eu-web-policy-skills/blob/main/skills/eu-cookie-policy/references/legal-basis.md) references.
  Check consumer and privacy rules independently when the site serves customers or monitors users outside the operator's establishment.
  For EU GDPR scope and transfers, use the [EDPB territorial scope guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/guidelines-32018-on-the-territorial-scope-of-the-gdpr-article-3-version-adopted_en) and the [European Commission's adequacy decisions](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/adequacy-decisions_en).
  Verify language and implementation requirements in each target market.

## Americas

- **United States.** Open the [United States guide](united-states.md) for federal rules, California and New York starting points, and the process for verifying other states.
- **Canada.** Check the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner's overview of federal and provincial privacy laws](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/02_05_d_15) and [Office of Consumer Affairs online shopping guidance](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/office-consumer-affairs/en/connected-consumer/online-shopping).
  Check the applicable province, the scope of federal law and any direct marketing and language requirements.
- **Brazil.** Check the current [General Personal Data Protection Law](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm), [ANPD cookie guidance](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/materiais-educativos-e-publicacoes/guia-orientativo-cookies-e-protecao-de-dados-pessoais.pdf) and [e-commerce Decree 7,962](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2011-2014/2013/decreto/d7962.htm).
  Check [ANPD international transfer rules](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/assuntos-internacionais/transferencia-internacional-de-dados), consumer remedies and Portuguese publication requirements.
- **Mexico.** Check the [Chamber of Deputies' consolidated private-sector personal data law](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFPDPPP.pdf), subsequent changes and the competent authority.
  Start consumer research with [Profeco online shopping guidance](https://www.gob.mx/profeco/documentos/compras-por-internet-recomendaciones).

## Asia and Oceania

- **Australia.** Check the [Australian Privacy Principles guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines), the scope of the Privacy Act and [ACCC online shopping guidance](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/buying-online).
  Verify which consumer guarantees, marketing or tracking rules apply.
- **New Zealand.** Start with the [Privacy Commissioner’s Privacy Act principles](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/) and the [Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment's consumer protection guidance](https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/).
  Verify the scope of collection notices, overseas disclosures and actual consumer remedies.
- **China.** Check the [Personal Information Protection Law](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_f374e8245320413181742e6d1baf4366.html) and [E-Commerce Law](https://www.samr.gov.cn/wljys/gzzd/art/2023/art_3e75434c954b4cb9bd14867dacff26d1.html).
  Verify applicable cross-border rules, local requirements and the authoritative language version.
- **India.** Check the [Digital Personal Data Protection Act](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf), the [Digital Personal Data Protection Rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Rules-2025%3B), applicable commencement notifications, and the [Department of Consumer Affairs legislation directory](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/consumer-protection-acts).
  Verify which provisions apply on the launch date.
- **Japan.** Check the [Personal Information Protection Commission legal directory](https://www.ppc.go.jp/en/legal/) and the [Consumer Affairs Agency mail-order guidance](https://www.no-trouble.caa.go.jp/foreignlanguage/english/mailorder/).
  Verify electronic communications and external transmission rules where relevant to tracking.
- **Singapore.** Check the [Personal Data Protection Act](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/PDPA2012) and the [Competition and Consumer Commission's fair-trading legislation and guidelines](https://www.ccs.gov.sg/consumer-protection/legislation-and-guidelines/).
  Verify notice, consent or other applicable conditions, and consumer obligations for the actual offer.
- **South Korea.** Check the national legislation database for the [Personal Information Protection Act](https://www.law.go.kr/lsInfoP.do?lsiSeq=270351&urlMode=engLsInfoR&viewCls=engLsInfoR) and [consumer e-commerce law](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsiSeq=260399&urlMode=engLsInfoR&viewCls=engLsInfoR).
  Confirm the current Korean texts and relevant regulator guidance before relying on translations.

## Middle East and Africa

- **United Arab Emirates.** Start with the [government data protection law directory](https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/data-protection-laws) and [government consumer protection guidance](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/justice-safety-and-the-law/consumer-protection).
  Verify which federal, emirate or free zone regime applies to each entity and flow.
- **Saudi Arabia.** Start with the [Saudi Data and AI Authority legal directory](https://sdaia.gov.sa/en/SDAIA/about/Pages/RegulationsAndPolicies.aspx) and [Ministry of Commerce regulations](https://mc.gov.sa/en/Regulations/pages/default.aspx).
  Check privacy and transfer rules, the applicable e-commerce law and required publication language.
- **South Africa.** Check the [Protection of Personal Information Act](https://www.justice.gov.za/legislation/acts/2013-004.pdf), current Information Regulator guidance, and the [Consumer Protection Act](https://www.gov.za/documents/consumer-protection-act).
  Verify electronic transactions, direct marketing and any sector rules where relevant.
- **Kenya.** Start with the [Data Protection Commissioner's legal directory](https://www.odpc.go.ke/data-protection-laws-kenya/index.html) and the [Competition Authority consumer protection guidance](https://cak.go.ke/consumer-protection).
  Verify applicable notices, tracking, transfers and any seller obligations.

If a market is unlisted or a source cannot be verified, apply the same research workflow.
Keep affected provisions pending until the current local rule and the site's implementation are verified.
