# United States market guide

Use this guide when the requested scope includes the United States.
Verify the federal and state rules in force for the site, audience and date of use.
Identify the relevant states from the requested scope and facts such as customer residence, offers, delivery and tracking.
For a nationwide request, document federal and state findings separately and list states or rules still awaiting verification.

## Federal starting points

- **Terms.** For paid online subscriptions, start with [15 U.S.C. § 8403](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/8403) and verify current federal and state rules on disclosures, informed agreement, billing and cancellation.
  Verify delivery and return requirements for the actual offer and check any sector-specific rules.
- **Privacy and cookies.** Verify the site's actual privacy promises and applicable federal laws, including [the FTC Act](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/45) and the [COPPA Rule](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312) where relevant to children.
  Check applicable rules for sector-specific data and direct marketing in the [U.S. Code](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/collection/USCODE).
  Verify state notice, data use, rights and tracking rules separately.

## What to verify before drafting

- For paid offers, record the seller, billing terms, signup, renewal notices, consent records and cancellation route.
  Check the actual flow, including a hosted checkout when applicable.
- For privacy, map data, purpose, recipient, retention, rights and request channels to the relevant law.
  Distinguish state-law rights from any rights the operator voluntarily offers more broadly.
- For cookies, trace what loads and what data each vendor receives before and after the applicable controls are exercised.
  Check any applicable requirements for targeted advertising, sale or sharing, or browser opt-out preference signals.
  Verify that privacy and cookie statements match the observed behavior.

Use a common section only when the verified facts and rights are shared.
Use state-specific sections or notices where the differences matter.
Keep a claim on hold if applicability or the corresponding site behavior remains unverified.
