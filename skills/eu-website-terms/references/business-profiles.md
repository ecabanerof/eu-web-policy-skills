# Business profiles for website terms

Select the profiles that match the actual offer.
Use the [terms template](../assets/terms-template.md) and verify the markets and consumer rules in [legal-basis.md](legal-basis.md).
Treat the examples as starting points for confirmed features.

## SaaS

**Ask about** the contracting entity, business or consumer audience, plan and billing cycle, trial conversion, renewals, changes, cancellation, support, and what happens to workspaces and files when access ends.
Ask for the actual order and cancellation screens or a redacted description.

**Draft** the scope of the plan, account and workspace responsibilities, billing and renewal, how cancellation takes effect, and any real export or deletion options.
Separate contractual cancellation, statutory withdrawal and refunds.
Verify what the customer receives, any support or service commitments, and the remedies that apply.
If the service qualifies under [Chapter VI of the EU Data Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2854/oj), check the switching, exit and contractual information duties.

Example for a plan with verified self-service cancellation.

> You can cancel your subscription in {{account settings}}.
> Cancellation takes effect {{verified timing}}, and access to your workspace continues until {{verified point}}.

## AI services

**Ask about** what the AI feature does, who supplies and operates it, who can use it, what users submit, what outputs they receive, and what the service promises.
Ask which uses the service supports or restricts and which interfaces display AI notices.

**Draft** the feature's scope, permitted uses, input permissions, output terms, and appropriate user review of results.
Verify the terms for submitted content and any rights or claims concerning the output.
Keep training or other secondary use distinct from the permissions needed to deliver the feature and reconcile those statements with the privacy notice.
Assess the relevant provider or deployer role and any disclosure, marking or labelling obligations under [AI Act Article 50](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act) where it applies.
Identify the interface where a required disclosure or label belongs.

Example for a verified writing assistant.

> The writing assistant generates draft suggestions from the text you submit.
> Review suggestions for accuracy and suitability before using them.

## E-commerce

**Ask about** who sells and receives payment, what buyers receive, where orders ship or services are delivered, pricing and fees, payment confirmation, delivery, cancellation, returns and support.
Identify the seller separately from a payment processor or marketplace.

**Draft** ordering, total price and charges, delivery or access, applicable conformity remedies, complaints and any return or withdrawal process.
Distinguish goods, services and digital content, and verify any product-specific exception.
Verify the customer information, order control and confirmation before relying on a checkout claim.
For a marketplace, establish which party owes each obligation and verify any applicable platform disclosures.

Example for goods dispatched by a verified seller.

> {{Seller}} accepts and fulfils your order.
> Before you place it, the checkout shows the total price, delivery charge and available delivery options.
